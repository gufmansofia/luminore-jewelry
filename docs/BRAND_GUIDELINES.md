## Introduction

This document contains the corporate identity guidelines for Luminore Jewelry, including logo, symbol, color palette, typography, and usage examples.

---

## About the Project

### Key Idea

Creation of individual jewelry of any complexity, diamonds of any sizes and colors. Unlike natural diamonds that cost hundreds of thousands of dollars, Luminore offers truly accessible, individual designs coordinated with the client, or ready-made models that are timeless classics.

### Brand Value

Creating jewelry that emphasizes individuality. The brand name implies light - we speak about the brilliance that a diamond gives and about the sparkle in your eyes when you get what you desire.

### Brand Mission

To reveal light and brilliance in people without any boundaries, be they financial or otherwise.

## Logo System

The Luminore visual identity consists of three main elements:

### 1. Sign (Symbol)

A geometric diamond illustration with detailed line work representing the facets and brilliance of a cut diamond.

- **Minimum size**: 10mm width
- **Usage**: Can be used independently for brand recognition on accessories, souvenirs, and when the brand name is clear from context

### 2. Name (Logotype)

**LUMINORE** - the brand name rendered in Cinzel typeface

- **Letter spacing**: 3%
- **Minimum size**: 25mm width

### 3. Slogan (Tagline)

**JEWELRY** - appears below the brand name in Cinzel typeface

### Logo Versions

| Version | Description | Use Case |
|---------|-------------|----------|
| **Full Version** | Sign + Name + Slogan | Primary usage for most applications |
| **Text Only** | Name + Slogan | When vertical space is limited |
| **Sign Only** | Diamond symbol | Favicons, small applications, merchandise |

### Logo Blocks

Symmetrical vertical logo blocks are the main variants. They have a calm appearance thanks to their symmetrical composition.

**Inversion**: The logo can be inverted (light on dark) for use on dark backgrounds.

## Logo Usage Rules

### Clear Space (Protection Zone)

To ensure readability and compositional clarity, the logo should be placed in free space away from other visual elements.

**Calculation method**: Divide the symbol perimeter into squares to get system module X. The minimum clear space equals two modules around the logo.

### Minimum Sizes

| Element | Minimum Width |
|---------|---------------|
| Sign (Symbol) | 10mm |
| Logotype | 25mm |

The minimum size is determined by:
- Print reproduction capability
- Digital readability

### Incorrect Usage (Do NOT)

The following modifications are prohibited:

- Changing proportions of logo and tagline
- Rotating the tagline
- Placing the sign in incorrect positions (side, below name, etc.)
- Adding shadows or gradients not specified in these guidelines
- Rotating the entire logo
- Stacking letters vertically
- Distorting or stretching the logo
- Using non-approved colors

## Typography

### Primary Typeface

**Cinzel** - Used for brand name and slogan

Cinzel is a typeface inspired by first century Roman inscriptions. It features elegant, classical letterforms with high contrast and refined serifs, perfectly suited for a luxury jewelry brand.

### Usage

| Application | Font |
|-------------|------|
| Brand Name (LUMINORE) | Cinzel |
| Tagline (JEWELRY) | Cinzel |

### Letter Spacing

- Brand name: **3% letter spacing**

## Visual Strategy

### Art Direction: Elemental Avant-Garde

The brand's visual language is based on a philosophy of contrasts. Sterile "studio" ideality gives way to artistic expressiveness, tactility, and a high-gloss atmosphere (High-End Editorial).

**Core aesthetic**: Dramatic collision of temperatures and textures. The warmth and softness of the product (velvet, rust suede, gold) are placed in a cold, austere, industrial environment.

### Key Visualization Principles

#### 1. "Fire in Ice" Concept

The brand color Luminore Rust (#D1642E) serves as the main source of visual warmth. Packaging or products should read as a hot, pulsating object in a cold environment.

**Implementation**: Use ice, meltwater, frost, glass, or metal as background environment.

#### 2. Materiality and Texture Conflict

Flat, "lifeless" backgrounds are excluded. Priority is given to expressed tactility and material juxtaposition:

- **Soft/Smooth**: Packaging texture (nubuck/suede), polished gold, gem facets
- **Raw/Rough**: Porous concrete, crumpled foil, raw stone, textured paper

#### 3. Chiaroscuro Lighting Drama

Instead of soft diffused light, use hard, directional lighting to create volume and drama:

- Clear, graphic shadows from objects
- Accent highlights and reflections on ice edges, foil, and precious stones
- Deep shadows using Dusty Amethyst (#564C5B) for emphasis

#### 4. Compositional Purity

Exclude any visual noise. Focus strictly on the subject and environment texture. Mandatory presence of "air" (negative space) around the object to create a sense of premium quality.

## Usage Examples

### Packaging Applications

- **Ring boxes**: Orange exterior with purple velvet interior, embossed logo on lid, diamond symbol on inside lid
- **Jewelry boxes (drawer style)**: Orange matte exterior with gray interior
- **Business cards**: Orange background with gray/silver embossed logo and sign

### Print Applications

- **Embossed stationery**: White textured paper with blind embossed logo and sign
- **Colored card stock**: Orange textured paper with foil stamped logo

### Signage

- **Store front signs**: Orange background with white/silver text
- **Window decals**: White or frosted vinyl on glass

### Merchandise

- **Stickers**: White circular stickers with black diamond symbol
- **Packing tape**: Cream/beige tape with repeating Luminore Jewelry pattern
- **Leather goods**: Embossed diamond symbol on black leather
